## Mathematical checks and corrections

The app preserves the scope, not copying errors from the key:

- 5.1 page 1, bottom-right example: y = 2x - 1 and x + y = 5 have slopes 2 and -1. They have ONE solution, (2, 3), despite the handwritten 'no solution' label. The app explicitly teaches that different slopes meet once; the intercepts need not be different.
- 5.2 handwritten page 4, problem 9 was checked at higher resolution: the intermediate equation is -18y = -72, consistent with y = 4 and (-6, 4). This is not an error in the notes.
- 5.2 handwritten page 3, second system: the exact y-coordinate is -49/6 (approximately -8.1667). A truncated decimal is not an exact equality. Original introductory app examples use exact, mostly integer solutions rather than this arithmetic-heavy case.
- 'Same numbers' for elimination means equal coefficient magnitudes. Adding cancels opposites; subtracting cancels equal coefficients. Subtraction affects EVERY signed term, including the right side.
